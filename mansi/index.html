Shubh Deepavali

Happy Diwali

Mansi

May your home glow with diyas, your heart with joy, and your year with sweet surprises.

Three wishes for you

Tap or hover each one and watch the sky answer.

Light

May every dark corner of your year fill up with warm, golden light.

Joy

May laughter find you often, loudly, and at the most unexpected moments.

Sweetness

May good news, good people and plenty of mithai come your way.

Light your diyas

Tap each diya to light it.

Shubh Deepavali,
dear Mansi

Made with love, light and a lot of sparkle
